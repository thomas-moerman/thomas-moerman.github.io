about

Hi, I’m Thomas.

  1. first desktop

    I can trace it all back to the day we got our first desktop at home. Within weeks I was asking questions nobody in the house could answer. Why did this PC choke on every game I wanted to play? And why was our internet so painfully slow?

  2. the it guy

    Help with the internet arrived in the form of an older colleague of my father, a man who introduced himself as “an IT guy”. I can still picture the long hairs growing out of his ears and the way he tugged at them while he stared at the screen. He clicked “Troubleshoot internet connection” ten times in a row, as if the eleventh try might be the lucky one. It never was. He left without fixing a thing, and that annoyed me so much that I sat down, started digging, and didn’t stop until it worked.

  3. underneath

    Fixing our internet was no big deal. What it left behind was. I suddenly wanted to know how the digital world around me actually worked, and I had learned that clicking until something works is never enough. I wanted to see what happens underneath. I still do.

  4. the army

    School was another story. I was not a good student. For years I blamed my surroundings, but the truth is that it was me, and that is how I ended up in the military. I liked it for a while, until I felt I no longer belonged there, so I left. I didn’t leave empty-handed, though. The army gave me a strong ethical compass and a discipline I still rely on every day.

  5. msc ai

    Leaving gave me an energy I hadn’t felt before, and for the first time in my life I turned out to be a good student. I went on to earn an MSc in Artificial Intelligence, and it led me straight back to that old urge to understand how things work underneath.

  6. honestly

    I have never been a great developer, and I won’t pretend otherwise. What I am good at is getting AI to do great things, and I believe in it completely. We have barely scratched the surface of what it can do, even if not everyone sees it yet.

  7. today

    Today I’m the CTO of Forenoon, a small startup. I want to build great things, keep learning, and help others along the way. That is pretty much what the kid behind our first desktop wanted too. The internet is just a lot better now.
